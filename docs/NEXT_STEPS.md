# Next steps

*Plain-language header: what later versions add, so v0.1.0 is understood as a first step.*

**v0.1.0 (December 2026).** Linked panel 2016 to 2025 for OSC entities; community energy for cities, towns and villages; NYPA projects; review log; DOI.

**v0.5 (target spring 2027).**
- County energy values (UER county dataset), and confirmation of town and village nesting.
- Fiscal-period alignment: energy summed over each entity's own fiscal months.
- Inflation-adjusted dollars.
- Comparison tables: each government against peers of similar class, population and energy use (the "bench" in MUNI-BENCH).

**v1.0 (target 2027).**
- Population and housing denominators from the Census American Community Survey.
- NYSERDA program participation layers (for example Clean Energy Communities).
- Cross-reference to the Verification Gap Ledger: which funding programs touched each government.
- Data descriptor paper.
